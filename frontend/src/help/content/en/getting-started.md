Monqom helps you keep track of income, expenses and budgets in a workspace.

1. [Create an account](/register) and follow the instructions to verify your email, then sign in.
2. Choose **Add transaction** and record an expense or income. Select a category and an account or wallet so you can find it later.

[![Add transaction button](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-transaction-add-light.png)](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-transaction-add-light.png)

3. Open **Dashboard** to see your monthly overview. Use the month selector to look at another month.

[![Dashboard showing a monthly spending overview and categories with fictional data](/marketing/screenshots/en/dashboard-light.png)](/marketing/screenshots/en/dashboard-light.png)

4. When you are ready, open **Budgets** and set a monthly limit for a category.

[![Add Budget button](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-budget-add-light.png)](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-budget-add-light.png)

Continue with [transactions](/help/en/transactions/) or [budgets](/help/en/budgets/). You can change your language and appearance in the app settings.
