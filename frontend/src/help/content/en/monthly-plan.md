Divide your planned income between expenses and savings goals.

## Plan income and savings


1. In **Budgets**, find **Monthly plan** and select **Enter planned income**.

[![Monthly plan with the Enter planned income button highlighted](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-plan-income-light.png)](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-plan-income-light.png)

2. Enter an amount manually or use income transactions. If using transactions, you can include all income categories or select specific ones.

[![Dialog for choosing the planned income source](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-plan-dialog-light.png)](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-plan-dialog-light.png)

[![Using transactions as the income source](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-plan-transactions-light.png)](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-plan-transactions-light.png)

3. Select **Save**. To change the source or amount later, select **Edit income**.

## Divide your income

1. Set [category budgets](/help/en/budgets/) for the expenses you want to plan. Their limits appear as **Planned expenses**.
2. Under **Savings goals**, select **Plan savings**.

[![Monthly plan with Plan savings highlighted](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-plan-allocate-light.png)](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-plan-allocate-light.png)

Enter this month’s amount for each goal and select **Save**. If you do not have a goal yet, [create one](/help/en/goals/) first.

[![Form for planning savings towards a goal](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-plan-goals-light.png)](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-plan-goals-light.png)

3. Check **Still to plan**. This is your income minus planned expenses and savings. You can assign the remainder to budgets or goals, or leave it unallocated.

For example, with PLN 5,000 of income, PLN 1,300 in budgets and PLN 500 in planned savings, PLN 3,200 remains to plan.

[![Income allocation with the remaining amount highlighted](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-plan-summary-light.png)](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-plan-summary-light.png)


If planned expenses and savings exceed your income, **Planned above income** shows the excess. Adjust budget limits, goal amounts or planned income to change the plan. Subcategory limits are included in their parent budget.

## Plans and actual savings

A planned amount is not a deposit. Once you set money aside, record the deposit separately in the [goal details](/help/en/goals/). The plan shows your progress against the amount planned for this month.

Income entered manually applies to the selected month. If you use transactions, the plan totals income from the selected categories for that month. Change the month in **Budgets** to review or prepare another plan.
