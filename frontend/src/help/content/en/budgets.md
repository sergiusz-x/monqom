A budget is a monthly spending limit for a category. The budget page compares it with recorded expenses so you can see what remains or how much you have exceeded it by.

## Set a limit

1. Open **Budgets** and choose the month.
2. Select **Add Budget**.

[![Budget page with Add Budget highlighted](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-budget-add-light.png)](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-budget-add-light.png)

3. Choose a category and enter a monthly limit.

[![Form for adding a budget](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-budget-form-light.png)](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-budget-form-light.png)

4. Under **Apply to**, choose whether the limit applies only to this month or also to future months. Then select **Create budget**. Recurring budgets use the workspace’s base currency.

[![Budget form with the period selector highlighted](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-budget-scope-light.png)](/marketing/screenshots/en/help-budget-scope-light.png)

[![Budget page with categories below and above their limits, using fictional data](/marketing/screenshots/en/budgets-light.png)](/marketing/screenshots/en/budgets-light.png)

Open a budget to see details and recent expenses or to change or remove the limit. Removing a budget does not remove your transactions. Spending in categories without a limit appears separately.

Learn how to divide your income in the [monthly plan guide](/help/en/monthly-plan/).
